SUMMARY / POWER CLAIM (v7)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary Δd′=0.5: >80% power at ICC ≤ 0.20 (range 0.821–0.868); 0.77–0.82
at ICC=0.30, consistent with the conservative rater-level paired estimator;
GLMM-based estimates expected to be higher given item-level information
pooling.
Secondary Δd′=0.25: underpowered (range 0.192–0.271); pre-registered as
exploratory (reported with effect-size CI, not a reject/retain decision).

Generative assumptions: baseline d′=1.0, unbiased criterion (optimistic for
d′ precision); estimator is rater-level paired d′ (conservative vs. the
crossed rater×item GLMM of Repair #3). 10,000 iterations/cell, seed=42.

RQ4 — LATITUDE × CONDITION INTERACTION POWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Design: 350 items × 20 raters × 2 conditions (within-subjects), continuous latitude.
DGP: mixed-effects logistic (item+rater random intercepts); f²=0.05 ⇒ interaction b3=0.640 (OR≈1.90 per SD latitude).
Analysis: item-clustered linear-probability Wald test, α=0.05, 1000 sims, seed=42
  (approximates the crossed rater×item logistic GLMM; statsmodels/lme4 unavailable).
Type-I error under H0 (b3=0): 0.048  (calibrated, ~0.05).
POWER at f²=0.05: 1.000  (item-level test is well-powered — resolves the vacuous n=4 Spearman).
